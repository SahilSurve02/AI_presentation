Automated Safety Alert System
Technical Overview

A fast, accurate, and multi-layered AI system that identifies dangerous content in school incident reports instantly, ensuring no threat goes unnoticed.

The 4-Step Safety Check

Every report goes through a rigorous multi-stage pipeline to ensure accuracy and reduce false alarms.

01

Duplicate Check

Instantly checks history to skip reports we've already seen. Saves time and resources.

02

Keyword Filter

Scans for 500+ dangerous patterns (weapons, self-harm, threats) using smart context matching.

03

Dual AI Review

Two independent AI models read the text. Both must agree on danger to raise an alert.

04

Expert Judge

If the AIs disagree, GPT-4o mini steps in as the expert judge to make the final call.
